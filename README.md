# Embark Showcase 🌍

Sneak peak of Embark, a new way to travel

### Discover experiences. Plan adventures. Share the journey.

Embark is a travel and social discovery application designed to bring exploration, trip planning, and social experiences together in one place.

From discovering new destinations and experiences to organizing itineraries and sharing adventures, Embark makes it easier to turn travel inspiration into real-world experiences.

**Status:** In Development · Private Beta

## ✨ Key Features

- **Experience Discovery:** Discover destinations and experiences to inspire your next adventure.
- **Trip Planning:** Organize travel ideas and build plans around your interests.
- **Interactive Itineraries:** Bring trip activities and experiences together in one organized view.
- **Social Discovery:** Explore travel through a more connected and community-driven experience.
- **Location-Based Discovery:** Leverage mapping and location services to explore places around the world.

## 🏗️ Engineering & Development

Embark is an independently developed application built with an emphasis on creating a cohesive, scalable mobile experience.

Key engineering areas include:

- Cross-platform mobile application architecture.
- Backend integration and data management.
- Authentication and user onboarding.
- Location-based services and mapping integrations.
- Media storage and image handling.
- Interactive application interfaces and user experiences.
- Integration of discovery, planning, and itinerary workflows.

## 👨‍💻 About the Project

Embark is an independent software project built to explore the intersection of travel, technology, and social discovery.

The goal is simple: make discovering places, planning adventures, and sharing experiences feel like one connected experience.

**Built independently from concept to implementation.**
